The Downward Spiral

Contemplating Sense Objects ↓ Attachment Develops ↓ Lust (Unfulfilled Desires) ↓ Anger Arises ↓ Delusion & Loss of Intelligence
↓

The Greatest Enemy

Effects of an Uncontrolled Mind:

  • Impure consciousness
  • Filled with lust, anger, and greed
  • Leads to bad habits & addictions
  • Causes unethical behavior
  • Creates enmity with others
  • Results in deep frustration
↓

The Best Friend

Benefits of a Controlled Mind:

  • Sharp focus & clarity
  • Emotional control
  • Deep inner peace
  • Equipoised in success and failure
  • Freedom from fear and anger
  • Realization of our true spiritual self
↓

The Solution

  • Association (Devotees)
  • Books (Gita & Bhagavatam)
  • Chanting (Holy Names)
  • Diet (Prasadam)
  • Engagement (Spiritual Service)
↓

Two Key Principles


  • 1. Detachment (Vairagya)
    Withdrawing senses from lower tastes.

  • 2. Practice (Abhyasa)
    Engaging the mind in a higher, spiritual taste.
"It is possible by suitable practice and by detachment." (BG 6.35)
