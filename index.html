Purushottama Adhika Masa

Things to be followed during this sacred month

🪷
Use Arrow Keys to Navigate ↓

P - U - R

  • P – Prayers:
    For inner purification, recite the prayers of Lord Brahmā (Canto 10, Chapter 14).
  • U – Upgrade Our Chanting:
    Improve the quality of your chanting and, where possible, increase the number of rounds.
  • R – Recitation:
    Regularly recite Chapter 15 of the Bhagavad Gītā.

U - S

  • U – Utsāha (Enthusiasm):
    Perform all services with sincerity and joyful enthusiasm.
  • S – Study Scriptures:
    Study Śrīmad Bhāgavatam and Bhagavad Gītā (especially Chapter 15). Sing bhajans glorifying Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa (such as Yugalāṣṭakam and Chorāṣṭakam).

H - O

  • H – Holy Dhāmas:
    Visit sacred dhāmas and bathe in holy rivers. If not possible, remember and chant the name of Mother Gaṅgā before bathing.
  • O – Offerings:
    Offer a ghee lamp to Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī and Lord Kṛṣṇa.

T - T

  • T – Tapasya (Austerity):
    Wake early and perform Maṅgala-ārati during Brahma-muhūrta.
  • T – Teaching & Sharing:
    Share the glories of Purushottama Māsa with others and encourage them to observe this sacred vrata.

A - M - A

  • A – Association of Devotees:
    Attend weekly classes and spiritual gatherings regularly.
  • M – Mercy Through Charity:
    Engage in charity and acts of kindness as an offering of devotion.
  • A – Avoid:
    Avoid bad association and unnecessary or unproductive talk.
